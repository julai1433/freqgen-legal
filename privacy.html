Privacy Policy

Effective September 3, 2026 App FreqGen — iOS Bundle ID dev.hanoi.freqgen

FreqGen is an audio signal generator. It has no account, no server, no ads, and no in-app purchases — it plays tones through your device's speaker and does nothing else. This is the short version of that in policy form.

No network access

FreqGen does not connect to the internet. It has no analytics SDK, no ad network, no crash reporter, and no backend of any kind. Every frequency, waveform, and setting you touch is computed and played locally on your device.

No accounts, no tracking

There's nothing to sign in to, nothing tied to your Apple ID beyond the App Store purchase record Apple keeps for every free download, and no identifier of any kind used to track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

FreqGen doesn't persist any settings between launches — every session starts from the same default tone. There is no local database, no UserDefaults, and nothing saved to disk beyond the app binary itself.

Children's privacy

FreqGen is listed under the Utilities category and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects nothing from anyone, this applies equally regardless of the user's age.

Changes to this policy — if that ever changes (for example, adding a feature that stores a setting locally), this page will be updated and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.
